# Azalea volunteer hours — activity catalog

Use this list when assigning or verifying volunteer hours (school service credit, chapter logs, letters of confirmation). Hours should reflect **real work time** (prep + activity + short wrap-up), logged with date, activity type, and a brief description.

**Not usually counted:** pure social hangouts with no task, commuting (unless your school allows it), or time already paid as a job.

---

## Hour categories you can offer

### 1. Advertising & outreach
Promoting Azalea programs, events, and partner opportunities.

Examples:
- Designing flyers, posters, or social posts
- Posting / scheduling Instagram, Discord, school newsletter blurbs
- Tabling at school or community fairs
- Inviting classmates; follow-up DMs or emails
- Sharing interest-form or event links with clear, accurate info

### 2. Creating art & messages
Making kind, person-first creative work for partners, caretakers, or campaigns.

Examples:
- Drawing / painting cards or thank-you art
- Decorating T-shirts for caretaker gifts (gift-ready only—no hate, profanity, or nudity)
- Helping with murals, banners, or event decorations
- Photo sorting / simple layout for approved journal or update posts (with permission)

### 3. Local & online program support
Showing up for recurring or one-off service with partners.

Examples:
- Learner’s Pursuit Fridays (online collaboration)
- Monthly in-person project / activity days with partners
- Helping set up, run, or clean up a local volunteer session
- Buddy / peer-support roles during inclusive activities (as trained)

### 4. Events & hospitality
Running Azalea-hosted gatherings.

Examples:
- Moon cake / kitchen events, camp days, meetups
- Check-in, materials table, activity stations
- Welcoming guests; accessibility / sensory support as assigned
- Packing kits or prep the day before an event

### 5. Translation & language support
Helping communication stay clear and respectful.

Examples:
- Translating short blurbs (EN ↔ 中文) for flyers, forms, or captions
- Helping at bilingual welcome tables
- Reviewing copy for tone (person-first, no pity language)

### 6. Website, media & documentation
Keeping Azalea’s story accurate and usable.

Examples:
- Writing short journal captions or update drafts
- Editing photos for approved posts (with consent rules)
- Helping film / edit short thank-you or event clips
- Light website content help under staff direction

### 7. Fundraising & admin support *(when a campaign is open)*
Back-office help—not collecting cash unless authorized.

Examples:
- Assembling donation materials or thank-you notes
- Tracking RSVPs / interest-form follow-ups in a shared sheet
- Printing, labeling, mailing (e.g. T-shirt project shipping prep)

### 8. Training & meetings
Prep that makes service safer and better.

Examples:
- Orientation / handbook review sessions
- CPR or safety trainings required for a role
- Planning meetings with a clear agenda and deliverable
- Interview / onboarding time for permanent members *(if you choose to count it)*

### 9. Taiwan / intensive program service
For accepted winter (or similar) cohort members—hours during the program window.

Examples:
- On-site companionship and daily activities with residents
- Rehearsal and performance for a closing play
- Partner-directed tasks while chaperoned
- Pre-trip meetings and required prep calls

## Suggested hour ranges (optional guide)

Use these only as **starting points**; adjust to actual time.

| Activity | Typical session |
| --- | --- |
| Single social post (design + publish) | 0.5–1.5 hr |
| Flyer / poster design | 1–3 hr |
| Learner’s Pursuit Friday | 1 hr (session) + prep if any |
| Local event shift | 2–4 hr |
| T-shirt decorate + pack/ship | 1–3 hr |
| Translation of a short page | 0.5–2 hr |
| Full event day (camp / kitchen) | 4–8 hr |
| Intensive program day on site | school/chapter policy (often full-day blocks) |

> [Name] volunteered with Azalea Initiative on [dates], completing approximately **[X] hours** in: [list 2–4 categories from above]. Duties included [one sentence]. Verified by [name / role], [email], [date].

---

## Notes for leads

1. Prefer **categories above** on forms so hours stay consistent across chapters.  
2. Permanent members and temporary volunteers can use the same catalog; intensity differs, not the activity names.  
3. School forms sometimes require a nonprofit contact—use the preferred inbox on the Contact page.  
4. Do not invent hours; if unsure, round down and describe the work.
