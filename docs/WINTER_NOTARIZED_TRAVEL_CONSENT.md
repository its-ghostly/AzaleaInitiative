# Notarized parental consent to travel — draft (Winter 2026–27)

**Status:** Draft for Azalea ops. Have a lawyer or experienced school-trip advisor review before use.  
**Use when:** Student is under 18 **and** no parent/guardian is traveling with the cohort.  
**Do not** store signed copies, passport scans, or IDs in this git repo.

---

## Form title

Parental / Guardian Consent for Minor to Travel Internationally — Azalea Winter Service Intensive (Taiwan)

## Instructions (for parents)

1. Complete every blank.  
2. Sign in front of a notary public in your state.  
3. Attach a clear copy of the signing parent/guardian’s government photo ID.  
4. Return the notarized original (or certified scan, if Azalea confirms) by the deadline in your acceptance email.  
5. Keep a copy for your records and for airline / immigration if asked.
