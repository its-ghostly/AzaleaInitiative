# Winter Service Intensive — ops packet outline

Working outline for Winter 2026–27. Aligns with `docs/WINTER_PROGRAM_FACTS.md`. Do not invent acceptance rates in parent or school materials.

## 1. Application packet

**Audience:** U.S. high school students (nationwide).

**Target cohort:** 8–16 students.

**Materials to collect**

1. Google interest form (see `docs/WINTER_INTEREST_FORM.md`)
2. Interview if invited
3. After acceptance: medical / emergency contacts, photo consent, code of conduct
4. **If under 18 and no parent/guardian is traveling:** notarized parental consent to travel (see `docs/WINTER_NOTARIZED_TRAVEL_CONSENT.md`) — due before airfare purchase / final roster
5. Optional: parent traveling as screened chaperone (background check required if counted in ratio)

**Process sketch**

1. Interest form → staff review
2. Interview window (invite only)
3. Decision + waitlist
4. Acceptance packet (handbook, medical, consent templates)
5. Notarized travel consent collected for eligible minors
6. Airfare booking guidance (program fee TBA)

**Copy rules**

- Use “limited / competitive cohort” language from facts doc only
- Never publish invented acceptance rates
- No Mandarin college course or language-credit pathway
- Parents are **not** required to chaperone; notarized consent enables under-18 travel with Azalea chaperones

## 2. Parent handbook (outline)

**Must cover**

- Program purpose and partner: 香園紀念教養院 (Hsiang Yuan)
- Dates window: Dec 23, 2026 – Jan 3, 2027 (exact travel days after acceptance)
- Cost: family-paid round-trip airfare (~USD 1,000–2,000 SFO ↔ Taipei); dorm + meals included on site; program fee TBA
- What is *not* promised: tax-deductible airfare, university credit, Mandarin college course / TOCFL
- **Parents need not travel.** Under-18 students without a traveling parent/guardian need a **notarized parental consent to travel** naming authorized Azalea chaperones
- Chaperone model: ~1 adult : 4–6 students; minimum 2 unrelated adults always (screened adults; parents optional)
- Duty of care: Azalea chaperones hold U.S. minor duty of care; Hsiang Yuan staff are partners, not substitutes
- Daily rhythm: exercise, walks, art, baking; closing play / performance
- Communication plan (emergency contacts, time zones, check-in cadence)
- Medical forms, insurance, medications, allergies
- Behavior / dignity / photo-consent expectations
- Packing list and tech rules
- Withdrawal / cancellation policy (TBD with fee structure)

**Attachments**

- Medical + emergency contact form
- Photo / media consent
- Code of conduct (person-first language)
- Notarized parental consent to travel (under 18, no parent on trip) — `docs/WINTER_NOTARIZED_TRAVEL_CONSENT.md`
- Itinerary draft (partner-confirmed)

## 3. Chaperone recruitment

**Ratio:** 1 adult per 4–6 students; never fewer than 2 unrelated adults with the group.

**Parents as chaperones:** optional. A parent may travel after screening if they wish; they are not required. Minors without a traveling parent use notarized consent + Azalea chaperones.

**Preferred roles (in order of parent trust)**

1. Credentialed teachers / counselors (background-checked)
2. Screened parents (optional; not the sole adult with the group; still need two unrelated adults overall)
3. Bilingual college assistants as *support* (not sole supervisors)

**Screening**

- Application + references
- Background check / fingerprinting as required by jurisdiction
- Youth-protection briefing + Azalea dignity guidelines
- First Aid / CPR strongly preferred before departure

**Duties**

- Flight and on-site supervision
- Night checks / rooming policy
- Partner liaison with Hsiang Yuan staff
- Incident reporting and parent communication
- Supporting the closing performance logistics
- Carry copies (or secure digital access) of notarized consents and medical authorizations for minors

**Recruitment channels**

- Past trip adults and teachers
- School counselor networks
- Parent interest after student acceptance (optional chaperone path; conflict-of-interest rules)
