# Winter Service Intensive — ops packet outline

Working outline for Winter 2026–27. Aligns with `docs/WINTER_PROGRAM_FACTS.md`. Do not invent acceptance rates in parent or school materials.

## 1. Application packet

**Audience:** U.S. high school students (nationwide).

**Target cohort:** 8–16 students.

**Materials to collect**

1. Interest form (site) → staff follow-up
2. Short essay: why serve with residents at 香園 / Hsiang Yuan
3. Activities / service list
4. One recommendation (teacher, counselor, or community mentor)
5. Parent / guardian acknowledgment (travel, medical consent later)
6. Optional Mandarin self-rating (not a gate)
7. Zoom interview (respectful service readiness, not language test)

**Process sketch**

1. Interest application on site → email to staff
2. Full packet invitation (deadline TBD)
3. Interview window
4. Decision + waitlist
5. Deposit / airfare booking guidance only after acceptance (program fee TBA)

**Copy rules**

- Use “limited / competitive cohort” language from facts doc only
- Never publish invented acceptance rates
- Mandarin tutoring: “exploring” only until confirmed

## 2. Parent handbook (outline)

**Must cover**

- Program purpose and partner: 香園紀念教養院 (Hsiang Yuan)
- Dates window: Dec 23, 2026 – Jan 3, 2027 (exact travel days after acceptance)
- Cost: family-paid round-trip airfare (~USD 1,000–2,000 SFO ↔ Taipei); dorm + meals supplied; program fee TBA
- What is *not* promised: tax-deductible airfare, college credit, confirmed Mandarin tutor, TOCFL
- Chaperone model: ~1 adult : 4–6 students; minimum 2 unrelated adults always
- Duty of care: Azalea chaperones hold U.S. minor duty of care; Hsiang Yuan staff are partners, not substitutes
- Daily rhythm: exercise, walks, art, baking; closing play / performance
- Communication plan (emergency contacts, time zones, check-in cadence)
- Medical forms, insurance, medications, allergies
- Behavior / dignity / photo-consent expectations
- Packing list and tech rules
- Withdrawal / cancellation policy (TBD with fee structure)

**Attachments (later)**

- Medical + emergency contact form
- Photo / media consent
- Code of conduct (person-first language)
- Itinerary draft (partner-confirmed)

## 3. Chaperone recruitment

**Ratio:** 1 adult per 4–6 students; never fewer than 2 unrelated adults with the group.

**Preferred roles (in order of parent trust)**

1. Credentialed teachers / counselors (background-checked)
2. Screened parents (not counted as sole supervisors for their own child if that breaks “two unrelated adults”)
3. Bilingual college assistants as *support* (not sole supervisors)

**Screening**

- Application + references
- Background check / fingerprinting as required by jurisdiction
- Youth-protection briefing + Azalea dignity guidelines
- First Aid / CPR strongly preferred before departure

**Duties**

- Flight and on-site supervision
- Night checks / rooming policy
- Partner liaison with Hsiang Yuan staff
- Incident reporting and parent communication
- Supporting the closing performance logistics

**Recruitment channels**

- Past trip adults and teachers
- School counselor networks
- Parent interest after student acceptance (with conflict-of-interest rules)
