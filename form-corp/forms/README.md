## Revision checks

- Articles packet: filing form revision 11/2024; bundled instructions may show an older date.
- SI-100: form revision 03/2022.
- SS-4: revision December 2025; use the included/current instructions.
- CT-1: revision 01/2024.

Form 1023 and 1023-EZ must be submitted through Pay.gov. This packet intentionally provides official electronic filing links in the main guide rather than presenting a paper copy as a valid application.

FTB 3500 is an alternative for organizations that cannot use 3500A; the main guide links to its official packet. Annual renewal/return forms are outside this startup packet.
